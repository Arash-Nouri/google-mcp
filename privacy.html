Privacy policy

Google MCP is used only by its owner, on their own computer, with their own Google accounts.

  • Mail it reads through the Gmail API is stored only on that computer. To summarize it and draft replies for the owner, parts of it are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI service.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
